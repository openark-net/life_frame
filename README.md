Life Frame is a completely free, open-source app designed around a simple idea: capture one photo every day to build a visual timeline of your life.

## Learn More

Visit [https://openark.net/life_frame/](https://openark.net/life_frame/) for detailed information about features, screenshots, and privacy policy.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions from anyone! Whether you want to fix bugs, add features, or improve documentation, your help is appreciated. Check out our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/openark-net/life_frame) to get started.
